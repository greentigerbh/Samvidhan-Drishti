# Digital Heritage Archive for Memorials, Manuscripts & Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

> **SIH Problem Statement: 26096**  
> **Digital Heritage Archive for Memorials, Manuscripts & Ambedkar**  
> **AI-Powered Institutional Archive and Audio-Visual Knowledge Platform**

---

## 📌 Overview

The **Digital Heritage Archive** is a hardware-software integrated platform designed to preserve, digitize, organize, search, and interact with historical and cultural heritage, with a primary focus on **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's manuscripts, writings, speeches, photographs, memorials, constitutional records, audio-video material, and institutional archival resources**.

Historical information is often distributed across physical documents, libraries, memorials, museums, and independent digital repositories. This fragmentation makes archival material difficult to discover, search, preserve, and access.

Our solution creates a unified digital archival ecosystem in which physical heritage can be transformed into **structured, searchable, contextualized, and preservable digital archival objects**.

### Core Capabilities

- 📷 Digital document acquisition
- 🔎 OCR-based digitization
- 🗂️ Metadata management
- 🔐 Digital preservation
- 🔍 Full-text and semantic retrieval
- 🔗 Knowledge mapping
- 🎧 Audio-visual archival management
- 🕰️ Interactive timelines
- 🏛️ Memorial and heritage storytelling
- 🤖 Evidence-grounded AI assistance
- 🖥️ Interactive archival kiosk
- 🌐 Multilingual accessibility
- 🏢 Institutional archival management

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Historical and cultural records related to **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar** and other heritage resources exist in multiple formats and across multiple institutions.

These include:

- Manuscripts
- Books and writings
- Speeches
- Photographs
- Constitutional debates
- Letters
- Audio recordings
- Video recordings
- Memorial information
- Historical documents
- Institutional records

### Key Challenges

1. **Fragmented archival resources**
2. **Limited discoverability**
3. **Physical deterioration of historical documents**
4. **Difficulty searching scanned documents**
5. **Lack of structured metadata**
6. **Separation of text, image, audio, and video archives**
7. **Limited multilingual accessibility**
8. **Difficulty establishing relationships between historical entities**
9. **Lack of accessible interactive archival interfaces**
10. **Risk of unsupported information generation when AI is used without source grounding**
